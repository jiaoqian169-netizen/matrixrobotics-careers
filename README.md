# We’re Hiring at Matrix Robotics 🚀

Matrix Robotics is looking for exceptional minds to join us in defining the future of Embodied AI and Robotics.

We are currently recruiting for three key roles:

## Open Positions

### Chief Scientist – Embodied AI (World Model)

Lead the research and development of next-generation world models and embodied intelligence systems, bridging AI foundation models with real-world robotic execution.

### Motion Control Algorithm Expert

Develop advanced real-time motion control systems for humanoid robots, including model-based control and reinforcement learning approaches.

### Dexterous Hand Design Expert

Design and develop state-of-the-art dexterous robotic hands, covering mechanism design, actuation, transmission, sensing, and system integration.

## What You’ll Do

You will help push the boundaries of physical AI by:

- Building next-generation world models for embodied intelligence

- Engineering state-of-the-art dexterous robotic hardware

- Solving complex challenges across algorithms, software, and hardware
